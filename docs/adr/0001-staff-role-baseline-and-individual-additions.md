# One supplied role with removable individual additions

The permission redesign gives each agency staff member one supplied role plus removable individual action or record-scope additions; agency administrators may edit Intake Specialist, Case Manager, and Operations baselines, while Admin and Dev remain protected. Agencies cannot create custom roles or combine roles for one person; this keeps inheritance understandable while allowing a selected person to have more access than peers. Removing an addition restores the role baseline and cannot deny an inherited permission.

Implemented locally behind reviewed organization activation. See [verification](../permission-upgrade-verification.md).
