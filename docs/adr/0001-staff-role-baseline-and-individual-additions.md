# One supplied role with removable individual additions

The permission redesign gives each agency staff member one platform-supplied role whose baseline permissions the agency can edit, plus removable individual permission additions. Agencies cannot create custom roles or combine roles for one person; this keeps inheritance understandable while allowing a selected person to have more access than peers. Removing an addition restores the role baseline and cannot deny an inherited permission.

Implementation is pending the remaining permission-design decisions.
