## Actor and setup differences

The main approval and rejection keepers use a separate same-organization requester, while the removed smoke tests request and resolve as the same admin. Both paths remain exercised. `match_access.authorize` applies the same approval permission and scoped participant checks to approve/reject; its requester-only branch applies to withdrawal. `status_change_request_service` passes the resolving actor to the same lifecycle commands, and `_approve_cancel`/`_restore_accepted` write status and party history independently of requester identity. The named self-resolution keepers protect successful access, while the stronger distinct-requester keepers assert the domain changes and actor attribution.

The stronger approval/rejection tests seed accepted prerequisites. `seed_surrogate_match` sets both parties to matched, the authenticated organization/owner, accepted match status, proposer/reviewer, and reviewed timestamp. It then invokes the real cancellation-request endpoint. Full HTTP proposal/accept/request/approval coverage remains in the open-attempt keeper and independent-session failure cases. The removed tests add no separate prerequisite transition contract.

The retry tests temporarily wrap the registry executor to fail after its real write, then restore the actual method before the successful retry. They do not substitute a fake executor or bypass `get_executor`; a missing or wrong action implementation would fail the retained persisted-result assertions. They are a stronger owner-boundary check than the removed implementation-class assertions.
