## 2024-05-18 - CI Failures Due to Dependency Vulnerabilities\n**Vulnerability:** Frontend CI scans occasionally fail because of missing or outdated `nanoid` packages introduced by transient dependencies (e.g., `postcss`).\n**Learning:** The pipeline fails the "Security Scans" job when high severity vulnerabilities are reported by `pnpm audit`.\n**Prevention:** Use `pnpm audit` directly and update affected packages in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` `overrides` section to force the secure version throughout the workspace.
