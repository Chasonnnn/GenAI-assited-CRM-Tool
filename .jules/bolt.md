## 2026-09-13 - Extracted and batched bulk media scans and token flushes
**Learning:** In bulk upload handlers, like `upload_media_assets` and `check_meta_tokens`, performing multiple small DB operations and calling background job enqueues per individual iteration in loops creates performance degradation via N+1 queries (`flush` and `scalar_one_or_none` in loops). For job enqueueing that performs a DB lookup, you need a vectorized/batched helper like `_ensure_media_scan_jobs_bulk`.
**Action:** Extract the keys into a list, perform a single bulk fetch using the `.in_()` operator outside the loop, use an in-memory dictionary for O(1) lookups during iteration, and batch enqueue external jobs by writing custom bulk enqueue functions. Also replace loop body `commit()` calls with `flush()` and defer the single `commit()` to the end of the operation.
